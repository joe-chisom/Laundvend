How it works

From choosing a service to receiving clean clothes, LaundVend keeps your laundry journey simple and visible.

1

Tell us what you need

Enter your pickup location, choose a service such as washing, ironing or folding, and select a convenient pickup time.

2

Choose your provider

Compare providers who match your location and schedule using availability, pricing, ratings, reviews and verification status.

3

Receive and track

Schedule your delivery window and follow your order as it moves from awaiting pickup to processing, ready and out for delivery.
