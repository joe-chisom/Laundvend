PROBLEM STATEMENT

Customers struggle to find reliable laundry providers who are actually available when needed.

I

Availability is difficult to see

A household may have clothes that need washing or ironing on a particular day, but finding a provider is a tiring task.

II

Pickup and delivery require coordination

Customers may need to coordinate how and when clothes will be collected and returned, adding friction to an otherwise convenient service.

III

Provider capacity is not visible

A provider may have free capacity but no simple way to make available service and pickup or delivery times visible to nearby customers.

THE GAP

A mismatch between demand and availability.

The research identifies a gap between people who need laundry services and providers who are available to provide them.
