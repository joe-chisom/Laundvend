# Flexbox Pod

## Flexible Laundry Services in Lagos

LaundVend is a proposed local laundry marketplace that connects households in
Lagos with laundry providers who are available to accept an order at a
customer's preferred time.

The project focuses on making laundry service availability, pickup and delivery
scheduling, pricing and provider trust information easier to discover in one
place.

> **Project status:** Research and product exploration

## Technology Stack

The project is currently being developed with:

- **HTML** — Page structure and content
- **CSS** — Styling, layout and responsive design
- **JavaScript** — Interactivity and application behavior

## Problem

Laundry services already operate across Lagos and commonly provide washing,
ironing, pickup and delivery. However, customers may still need to make several
phone calls or send multiple messages to find a provider who can:

- Serve their location
- Accept the order at the required time
- Collect the clothes conveniently
- Return them when needed
- Clearly communicate the total cost

At the same time, laundry providers may have available capacity but no simple
way to make their availability and pickup or delivery windows visible to nearby
customers.

## Proposed Solution

LaundVend explores a laundry marketplace where availability and scheduling are
central to the search experience. Customers could enter their location, select
a laundry service and choose pickup and delivery windows. The platform would
then show providers who match those requirements.

### Example Search

```text
Location: Lekki
Pickup: Today, 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM
Service: Wash & Iron
Delivery: Tomorrow, 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM
```

## Core Features

- Search for providers by location and service area
- Filter providers by real-time or scheduled availability
- Select washing, ironing, folding and other laundry services
- Schedule a preferred pickup time
- Schedule a preferred delivery time
- Compare laundry prices and pickup or delivery fees
- View provider photos and verification status
- Review provider experience, ratings and customer reviews
- Track orders through each stage of fulfillment

## Provider Listings

Each provider listing could include:

- Provider or business name
- Photo
- Services offered
- Location and service area
- Current and upcoming availability
- Pickup and delivery time windows
- Experience
- Rating and reviews
- Laundry price
- Pickup or delivery fee
- Verification status
- Request or booking button

## Order Journey

1. **Choose a location** — Enter a pickup address or service area.
2. **Choose a service** — Select washing, ironing, folding or another service.
3. **Choose a pickup time** — Select a convenient collection window.
4. **Choose a provider** — Compare availability, pricing, ratings and reviews.
5. **Schedule delivery** — Select when the cleaned clothes should be returned.
6. **Track the order** — Follow the order from awaiting pickup to processing,
   ready and out for delivery.

## Target Users

### Households

People who need help with clothes, bedding and other household items and prefer
convenient, scheduled pickup and delivery.

### Laundry Providers

Independent laundry professionals and laundry businesses that want to make their
services, available capacity and pickup or delivery windows visible to
potential customers.

## Research Findings

Our research identified six priorities for a convenient laundry marketplace:

- **Availability:** Customers need an easier way to find providers who can
  accept an order at a specific time.
- **Location:** Customers need providers who serve their area or offer pickup
  and delivery.
- **Trust:** Customers need information that helps them feel confident handing
  over their clothes.
- **Pricing:** Customers need clear laundry, pickup and delivery costs.
- **Convenience:** Scheduled pickup and delivery can remove the need to travel
  to a laundry shop.
- **Quality:** Customers need confidence that their clothes will be handled
  carefully, according to their instructions and returned on time.

## Trust and Quality Signals

Potential trust signals include:

- Provider or business photo
- Verification status
- Service experience
- Customer ratings and reviews
- Location
- Pickup and delivery options
- Service history

## Pricing Considerations

Laundry prices may vary based on:

- Number or weight of clothes
- Type of service
- Ironing
- Special-care items
- Pickup and delivery
- Customer location

Clearly displaying the laundry cost and any additional pickup or delivery
charges can reduce uncertainty before a customer submits a request.

## Identified Opportunity

Existing laundry services already address booking, pickup and delivery, pricing
and customer service. Flexbox Pod's opportunity is to make provider availability
and scheduling a central part of the search experience, helping customers find
providers who can meet both their location and timing requirements.

## Project Goals

- Make available laundry providers easier to discover
- Reduce the time spent coordinating through calls and messages
- Make pickup and delivery times easier to schedule
- Improve pricing transparency
- Help customers compare providers with useful trust signals
- Help providers expose available capacity to nearby customers

## Conclusion

There is an active market for convenient, on-demand laundry services in Lagos.
Flexbox Pod aims to simplify the connection between households and laundry
providers by focusing on availability, location, trust, service quality,
pricing and scheduled pickup and delivery.
