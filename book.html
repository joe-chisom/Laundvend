What happens next

  • Verified vendors in your area who are free in that window see your request.
  • You receive the vendor's name, rating and the full price including delivery.
  • Nothing is collected until you confirm, and the price is agreed before pickup.
  • Items are counted and logged at pickup, so any issue can be traced.
