Guide & FAQ

Everything worth knowing before you book.

Short, direct answers on how pickup, delivery, pricing and care work.

Getting started

How does LaundVend work?

Choose your area, the service you need and a pickup window. You then compare only the vendors who are available for that time, book one, and hand over your laundry when they arrive.

Which areas do you cover?

LaundVend is starting in Lekki, Yaba, Ikeja and Surulere. Select your area on the booking form and you will only see vendors who collect and deliver there.

Do I have to create an account?

No. A booking needs your name, phone number, email and pickup address so the vendor can reach you and find you.

Pickup & delivery

How long does a full cycle take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours from pickup to delivery. Vendors offering a faster window show it on their listing.

Do I need to be home for pickup and delivery?

No. You can leave the laundry with a gateman, a receptionist or in an agreed spot. Add the details in the care instructions box when you book.

Can I change my pickup time after booking?

Yes, up to a few hours before the scheduled window. The vendor is notified so the slot can go back into their availability.

What if a vendor cannot make my window?

The request goes to other verified vendors in your area who are free at that time. You are never left waiting on one vendor to reply.

Care & trust

How do I know a vendor is reliable?

Every listing shows verification status, years of experience, a rating out of five and customer reviews. Only verified vendors can appear in the results.

What happens if something is lost or damaged?

Items are counted and logged at pickup, so an order can be traced rather than guessed at. Anything missing or damaged is raised with the vendor against that record.

Can I ask for a specific detergent or a fragrance-free wash?

Yes. Add any care instruction when you book, including allergy-friendly, fragrance-free, hand-wash only or do-not-tumble-dry items.

What should I not send?

Leather, suede, heavily beaded garments and anything without a care label should be discussed with the vendor first, as standard washing can damage them.

Pricing & payment

How is the price worked out?

Listings show a starting price for a standard load plus the delivery fee. The final amount depends on weight or item count, special-care items and your distance, and is confirmed before pickup.

Why do two vendors charge different amounts?

Vendors set their own rates based on their service, equipment and area. Showing the laundry price and delivery fee together lets you compare the real total rather than a headline figure.

When do I pay?

After the price is confirmed and before the clothes are returned. Nothing is charged for a request that no vendor accepts.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge any time before pickup. Once your laundry has been collected the order is already in progress and cannot be cancelled.
