Guide & FAQ

Everything you need to know before you book a pickup — straight answers, no filler.

General

How does Laundvend work?

Book a pickup, hand over your laundry, and get it back washed, dried, and folded — no trip to a laundromat, no line to wait in.

What areas do you cover?

Enter your address when you book — if we cover your area, you'll see available pickup slots right away.

Pickup & Delivery

How long does a full cycle take?

Standard turnaround is 24–48 hours from pickup to delivery. Express options are shown at booking if you need it sooner.

Do I need to be home for pickup and delivery?

No. You can leave laundry with a doorman, reception, or in an agreed spot — just note it when you book.

Can I change my pickup time after booking?

Yes, up to a few hours before the scheduled pickup. Reschedule directly from your booking confirmation.

Care & Trust

What if something gets lost or damaged?

Every order is logged item by item at pickup, so any issue can be traced back and resolved directly with you — not guessed at.

Can I request specific detergents or a no-fragrance wash?

Yes — add any care instructions at booking, including allergy-friendly or fragrance-free options.

Payment & Pricing

How is the price worked out?

Priced by weight or by item, shown clearly before you confirm your booking — no surprise charges at delivery.

When do I pay?

Payment is taken online at booking, so there's nothing to sort out at pickup or delivery.

Can I cancel and get a refund?

Yes, if you cancel before pickup. Once your laundry has been collected, cancellations aren't refunded.
