Order stages

You always know where your laundry is.

An order moves through six stages. Each one has a name you can point at, so "where are my clothes?" has a clear answer at any moment.

  1. Stage 1 Awaiting pickup

    The booking is confirmed and the vendor is scheduled to collect.

  2. Stage 2 Picked up

    Items are counted and logged at collection so nothing is guessed at later.

  3. Stage 3 Processing

    Clothes are washed, dried, ironed or folded to your instructions.

  4. Stage 4 Ready

    The order is finished and waiting for its delivery window.

  5. Stage 5 Out for delivery

    The vendor is on the way back to your address.

  6. Stage 6 Delivered

    Clothes are returned and you can rate the vendor.
