The problem

Laundry is available in Lagos. Knowing who is free is the hard part.

Vendors already wash, iron and deliver. What customers cannot see is whether a vendor can take their clothes at the time that suits them, so every booking still starts with a round of calls and messages.

Availability is invisible

A vendor's opening hours say nothing about whether they have capacity for your load today.

Pickup needs coordinating

Agreeing when clothes are collected and returned takes several messages before anything is confirmed.

Spare capacity goes unused

Vendors with a free afternoon have no simple way to show nearby customers that they are open for work.
