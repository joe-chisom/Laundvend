Problem statement

Lagos does not lack laundry vendors. It lacks visible availability.

Washing, ironing, pickup and delivery are already offered across the city. What a customer still cannot see is which vendor can take their clothes at the time they actually need. That single missing fact is what turns a simple errand into a round of calls and messages.

I. Availability is hard to see

A household needs washing or ironing on a particular day. Opening hours do not answer whether a vendor has capacity that day, so the only way to find out is to ask, one vendor at a time.

II. Pickup and delivery need coordination

Even after a vendor says yes, when clothes are collected and when they come back has to be negotiated separately, adding friction to a service sold on convenience.

III. Spare capacity stays hidden

A vendor may be free this afternoon and have no way to tell nearby customers. Demand and supply exist at the same moment and never meet.

The gap

A mismatch between demand and availability.

Our research followed a customer from need to service. The delay is not in the washing. It is in everything that has to happen before a vendor is confirmed.

Research findings

Six things customers told us they need.

Availability

An easier way to find a vendor who can accept the order at a specific time.

Location

Vendors who serve their area, or who collect and deliver to it.

Trust

Enough information to feel confident handing over their clothes.

Pricing

Clear laundry, pickup and delivery costs before committing.

Convenience

Scheduled pickup and delivery that removes the trip to a laundry shop.

Quality

Confidence that clothes are handled to instruction and returned on time.

Opportunity

Make availability and scheduling the centre of the search.

Instead of searching broadly and then asking whether a vendor is free, a customer enters their location, pickup window and delivery window first, and sees only the vendors who match.

Target user

Households

People with clothes, bedding and household items to wash who would rather have them collected and returned at a time they chose than travel to a laundry shop and wait.

Target user

Laundry vendors

Independent laundry professionals and small laundry businesses who want their services, free capacity and delivery windows visible to customers nearby.

Our contribution

Connect a customer with a laundry vendor who is available at the time they need. Focusing on availability, location, trust, quality, pricing and scheduled delivery makes laundry simpler to arrange for households and easier to fill for vendors.
